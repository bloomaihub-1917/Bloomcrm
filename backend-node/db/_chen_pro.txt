Co-Founder & Principal Architect
2014 – Present
Yen Partnership Architects (十彥建築師事務所)  |  Taipei, Taiwan
Leads an award-winning multidisciplinary practice engaged in public architecture, mixed-use development,
industrial heritage adaptive reuse, and sustainable urban design. The practice operates extensively within
Taiwan's public procurement framework, with a track record of successful design competition entries and
government-tendered projects across civic, educational, cultural, and infrastructure typologies. Key heritage
and mixed-use project highlights:
▪ Lintianshan Forestry Cultural Park, Hualien — 152,398 m² (15.24 ha) Japanese colonial-era industrial
heritage site; master planning through Phase 3 regeneration; integrated program of cultural exhibition,
commercial hospitality, and community facilities; adaptive reuse revealing successive historical layers
including pre-colonial indigenous occupation. Featured in The Architectural Review.
▪ National Railway Museum, Taipei — In progress; 167,900 m² (16.79 ha) former Taipei Railway Workshop
(est. 1935), Taiwan's largest train maintenance facility; designated national monument 2015. Project
encompasses South Visitor Center and Phase 2 regeneration including adaptive reuse of the erecting shop (
組立工廠); large-scale urban industrial heritage adaptive reuse in central Taipei.
▪ Hsinpu Elementary School, New Taipei City — Renovation and extension of existing primary school
campus; 26,940 m² GFA; new building structurally integrated with existing classrooms; program includes
gymnasium, dance studio, library, courtyard garden, sky garden, sports field, assembly platform,
stormwater detention pond, and basement parking.
